THE PROBLEM

Renting Heavy Gear Locally Is Still a Hassle

Buying machines is too expensive for smaller jobs, and finding an available unit nearby usually takes too much back-and-forth.

  • High upfront cost: Buying excavators, backhoes, or dump trucks takes way too much capital for smaller contractors and builds.
  • Short-term project needs: Half the time, a job only needs a unit for a few days—not a permanent purchase.
  • Scattered Facebook listings: Local units are posted across different FB pages and groups, so checking who is actually free takes digging and messaging around.
  • Unclear rates and mobilization: Prices, hourly/daily terms, and distance-based mobilization fees often aren't clear until you DM back and forth.
  • Idle units on the owner side: Equipment owners lose out when machines sit parked with no steady booking flow.
